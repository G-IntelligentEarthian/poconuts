Farm-to-Container Quality Control

Every coconut exported by PoConuts undergoes a rigorous 5-stage quality lifecycle designed to withstand deep-sea maritime transit.

Pollachi Sourcing Integrity

The 5-Stage Export Quality Assurance System

Maintaining export grade consistency is essential for commercial importers and processing plants. Here is our calibrated protocol from plantation harvest to container door seal.

🌴
Stage 1

1. Selective Harvesting at Maturity

Trained climbers harvest only fully matured bunches (55–65 days) when kernel thickness has achieved maximum density and sweet water volume. Immature nuts with soft jelly meat are strictly excluded.

Parameters: 55–65 Days • Thick Copra • Audible Water Swash
🛡️
Stage 2

2. Precision De-Husking & Tuft Protection

Husks are manually stripped leaving a tight, uniform fiber tuft over the three germinative eyes to insulate against microbial intrusion, impact damage, and moisture loss during tropical shipping.

Protective Barrier: Tuft Retained over 3 Eyes
⚖️
Stage 3

3. Calibrated Weight Sorting (400g–550g)

Electronic scale sorting discards immature nuts, undersized specimens (<400g), oversized nuts (>550g), and micro-cracked shells. Target batch average: 450g nominal weight.

Standard Band: 400g – 550g (Nominal 450g)
🔬
Stage 4

4. Phytosanitary Inspection & Fumigation

Consignments undergo quarantine inspection by Plant Protection authorities and destination-compliant fumigation (Phosphine / Methyl Bromide) prior to port staging.

Compliance: Central Plant Quarantine & MoCCAE / NPPO Standards
❄️
Stage 5

5. Temperature-Controlled Reefer Transit

Pre-cooled marine containers maintain 13°C–15°C with 10–15% continuous fresh air ventilation to eliminate condensation and mold formation during ocean freight to Jebel Ali, Cat Lai, and Haiphong.

Settings: 13°C–15°C (55°F–59°F) • 10–15% Vent Exchange
