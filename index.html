About PoConuts

Connecting Pollachi's Heritage to Global Markets

“Started by [X] and [Y] to bring authentic, farm-grown coconuts from Pollachi to international markets, connecting the region's traditional coconut farming with buyers who value quality and traceability.”

Nestled in the agricultural heartland of Tamil Nadu, Pollachi is renowned worldwide for coconuts of exceptional copra density, natural sweetness, and long shelf life. For generations, local farming families have cultivated coconut groves fed by pristine Western Ghats runoff.

PoConuts bridges these traditional farming communities with commercial buyers and food processors in the UAE and Vietnam. By standardizing calibration, retaining protective eye tufts, and managing strict maritime logistics, we ensure that every nut arrives at foreign ports in peak condition.
